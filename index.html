What is the Pythagorean Theorem?

The Pythagorean theorem describes the relationship between the three sides of a right-angled triangle. The two shorter sides are called a and b, while the longest side, opposite the right angle, is called c.

a² + b² = c²

This means that the square of side a plus the square of side b is equal to the square of side c.

Catheti and Hypotenuse

Cathetus a

Side a is one of the two catheti. It forms the right angle together with side b.

Cathetus b

Side b is the second cathetus. It also forms the right angle together with side a.

Hypotenuse c

Side c is the hypotenuse. It is always opposite the right angle and is the longest side.
